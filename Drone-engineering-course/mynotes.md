# Drone Engineering Notes 🚁

## Flight Controller (FC)

The Flight Controller (FC) is the brain of the drone.

Functions:

* Receives sensor data
* Processes flight information
* Controls motor speed through ESCs
* Maintains stability during flight

Examples:

* Pixhawk
* SpeedyBee
* Betaflight FCs

---

## Electronic Speed Controller (ESC)

ESC stands for Electronic Speed Controller.

Functions:

* Controls BLDC motor speed
* Receives commands from FC
* Converts DC battery power into three-phase signals for BLDC motors

Why Important?

* Precise motor control
* Stable flight
* Fast response

---

## Brushless DC Motor (BLDC)

BLDC motors are widely used in drones because they provide:

* High efficiency
* High speed
* Low maintenance
* Long lifespan
* Better power-to-weight ratio

Important Term:

* KV Rating = RPM per Volt

Example:
1000 KV motor with 10V supply ≈ 10,000 RPM (no load)

---

## Propellers

Propellers generate lift by pushing air downward.

Functions:

* Create thrust
* Control drone movement
* Affect efficiency and flight time

Types:

* Clockwise (CW)
* Counter-Clockwise (CCW)

---

## Battery

Most drones use LiPo batteries.

Advantages:

* High energy density
* High discharge rate
* Lightweight

Important Terms:

* Voltage (V)
* Capacity (mAh)
* C Rating

---

## Power Distribution Board (PDB)

Functions:

* Distributes battery power
* Supplies power to ESCs
* Provides power to onboard electronics

---

## Frame

The frame acts as the skeleton of the drone.

Functions:

* Supports components
* Provides structural strength
* Maintains balance

Common Materials:

* Carbon Fiber
* Plastic
* Aluminum

---

## Carbon Fiber

Carbon Fiber is commonly used in drone frames.

Advantages:

* Lightweight
* Strong
* Corrosion resistant
* High stiffness

Why Used?
Provides strength without significantly increasing weight.

---

# Sensors Used in Drones

Sensors allow the drone to understand its surroundings and maintain stable flight.

---

## IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit)

IMU combines:

* Accelerometer
* Gyroscope

Functions:

* Measures movement
* Measures orientation
* Helps stabilize the drone

Without IMU:
Stable flight is extremely difficult.

---

## Gyroscope

Measures rotational motion.

Functions:

* Detects angular movement
* Helps maintain orientation
* Assists flight stabilization

Measures:

* Roll
* Pitch
* Yaw

---

## Accelerometer

Measures acceleration forces.

Functions:

* Detects tilt
* Detects movement
* Helps determine orientation

---

## GPS

Global Positioning System.

Functions:

* Position tracking
* Navigation
* Return-To-Home (RTH)
* Autonomous flight

---

## Magnetometer

Acts as a digital compass.

Functions:

* Detects Earth's magnetic field
* Determines heading direction
* Improves navigation accuracy

---

## Barometer

Measures atmospheric pressure.

Functions:

* Altitude estimation
* Height hold
* Stable hovering

---

## LiDAR

LiDAR = Light Detection and Ranging.

Working:

* Emits laser pulses
* Measures reflected light

Functions:

* Obstacle detection
* Terrain mapping
* Altitude measurement

Advantages:

* High accuracy
* Precise distance measurement

---

## Radar

Radar = Radio Detection and Ranging.

Working:

* Uses radio waves
* Measures reflected signals

Functions:

* Long-range detection
* Object tracking
* Navigation assistance

Advantages:

* Works in fog
* Works in rain
* Works in low visibility

---

## Infrared Sensor

Uses infrared radiation.

Functions:

* Object detection
* Obstacle avoidance
* Distance measurement

Advantages:

* Low power consumption
* Fast response

---

# Communication Protocols

## I2C

Inter-Integrated Circuit.

Features:

* Two-wire communication
* SDA (Data)
* SCL (Clock)

Applications:

* Sensors
* Displays
* IMUs

---

## SPI

Serial Peripheral Interface.

Features:

* High-speed communication
* Multiple devices support

Applications:

* Flight Controllers
* Sensors
* Memory Devices

---

## CAN Protocol

Controller Area Network.

Features:

* Reliable communication
* Noise resistant
* Long-distance communication

Applications:

* Industrial drones
* UAV systems
* Automotive electronics

---

# Drone Maintenance

## IPA (Isopropyl Alcohol)

Used for:

* Cleaning PCBs
* Removing flux residues
* Cleaning electronic components

Advantages:

* Evaporates quickly
* Leaves no residue
* Safe for electronics when used properly

# Fundamental Forces of Flight

## Lift

Upward force generated by propellers.

## Weight

Downward force caused by gravity.

## Thrust

Forward force generated by propulsion.

## Drag

Air resistance opposing motion.

Stable flight occurs when these forces remain balanced.
