# GPS in Drones

## What is GPS?

GPS (Global Positioning System) helps drones determine their position using satellites.

## Why is GPS Important?

* Navigation
* Position Hold
* Return to Home (RTH)
* Route Tracking

## Observation

Without GPS, drones can drift more easily due to wind and instability.

---

# BLDC Motor

## What is a BLDC Motor?

BLDC stands for Brushless DC Motor.

It converts electrical energy into rotational motion.

## Why Drones Use BLDC Motors

* High speed
* High efficiency
* Less friction
* Less heat generation
* Better lifespan

## Observation

Higher KV motors spin faster but usually provide lower torque.

---

# ESC (Electronic Speed Controller)

## What is ESC?

ESC controls the speed of the drone motors.

It receives signals from the flight controller and adjusts motor speed.

## Functions of ESC

* Controls motor RPM
* Maintains stability
* Helps drone movement
* Synchronizes motor response

## Observation

If one ESC behaves differently, the drone may become unstable.

# LiPo Battery

## What is a LiPo Battery?

LiPo stands for Lithium Polymer battery.

It provides high power output for drones.

## Important Safety Rules

* Never overcharge
* Never puncture
* Avoid overheating
* Monitor voltage carefully

## Observation

Larger batteries increase flight time but also increase drone weight.

# Yaw, Pitch, Roll, and Throttle

## Yaw

Yaw rotates the drone left or right.

## Pitch

Pitch moves the drone forward or backward.

## Roll

Roll tilts the drone left or right.

## Throttle

Throttle controls altitude by changing motor speed.

## Observation

Smooth control inputs improve drone stability and movement precision.

---

# Flight Controller

## What is a Flight Controller?

The flight controller is the brain of the drone.

It processes sensor data and controls motor movement.

## Main Responsibilities

* Stabilization
* Flight control
* Sensor processing
* Communication with ESCs

## Observation

A properly calibrated flight controller improves drone performance significantly.

# Drone Safety

## Pre-Flight Checks

* Check battery level
* Inspect propellers
* Verify GPS signal
* Confirm weather conditions
* Check controller connection

## Post-Flight Checks

* Disconnect battery safely
* Inspect drone body
* Check motor temperature
* Review any flight issues

## Observation

Safety checks reduce the chance of crashes and hardware damage.

# Weather and Drone Flying

## Why Weather Matters

Weather directly affects drone stability and safety.

## Important Factors

* Wind speed
* Rain
* Humidity
* Temperature
* Visibility

## Observation

Strong winds make drone control difficult, especially for lightweight drones.

# Payloads in Drones

## What is a Payload?

Payload refers to the additional weight carried by the drone.

Examples:

* Cameras
* Sprayers
* Sensors
* Delivery packages

## Observation

Increasing payload reduces flight time and increases power consumption.

## Observation

# Fundamental Forces Acting on a Drone

## Lift

Upward force generated by propellers.

## Weight

Downward force caused by gravity.

## Thrust

Forward movement force.

## Drag

Air resistance opposing movement.

## Observation

Stable flight happens when these forces remain balanced.
