# Drone Engineering Course - Daily Learning Log 🚁

This document records my day-by-day learning journey throughout the Drone Engineering Course.

---

## Day 1 - Basic Soldering

Learned the fundamentals of soldering and desoldering electronic components and proper handling of soldering equipment.

---

## Day 2 - Drone Components

Learned about the major components used in drones and their functions.

Topics covered:

* Battery
* ESC (Electronic Speed Controller)
* Flight Controller (FC)
* Motors
* Propellers
* Frame
* Power Distribution Board (PDB)

---

## Day 3 - Drone Frames

Learned about:

* Types of drone frames
* Frame selection criteria
* Importance of frame design in drone performance

## Day 4 - Fundamental Forces of a Drone

Learned about:

* Lift
* Weight
* Thrust

* Pitch
* Roll
* Yaw
* Throttle

* Propeller types and functions
